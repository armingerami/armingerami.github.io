01 About

Rigorous models, engineered to run fast.

I'm a Computer Science Ph.D. candidate at the University of Maryland, advised by Prof. Ramani Duraiswami. My research targets the computational efficiency of Transformers — sub-quadratic and linear attention, KV-cache reduction, and quantization — and their application to noisy, high-frequency domains such as financial markets.

My background in electrical engineering and signal processing shapes how I approach data: as signals with structure, noise, and latency budgets. I write the model, the GPU kernel, and the backtest.

ML for Markets

Feature extraction from high-frequency market data, gradient boosting and Transformer models for short-horizon price prediction, and backtesting.

Efficient Transformers

Linear and mixture-of-Gaussians attention, sublinear long-context decoding, and KV-cache compression for LLM serving.

High-Performance Computing

Hand-tuned CUDA kernels, hardware/software co-design for sparse linear algebra, and differentiable programming.
